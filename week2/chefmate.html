Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati rice
  • 500g Chicken (cut into pieces)
  • 2 Onions (sliced)
  • 2 Tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup Yogurt
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Red chili powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • 2 cups Water
  • 2 tbsp Ghee or Oil
  • Salt to taste

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse and soak the basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, fry onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté until fragrant.
  4. Add tomatoes, yogurt, and spices. Cook until the oil separates.
  5. Add chicken and cook until tender.
  6. Boil water, add soaked rice, and cook until half done.
  7. Layer rice over the chicken mixture, cover, and let it steam for 15 minutes.
  8. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.
